# Periodic

The periodic table of the 118 elements, in pixel art, with a quiz.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Controls

- **Hover** a cell to see its name and mass; **click** for its card.
- **Arrows**: move · **Q**: quiz · **Escape**: show everything.
- Click a **family** in the legend to isolate it.
- **Search** by name, symbol or number.

## The quiz

An element name appears: click its cell (symbols are hidden). Ten questions; your best score is kept.

## Data and sync

Quiz records are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on. Element names are in French.
